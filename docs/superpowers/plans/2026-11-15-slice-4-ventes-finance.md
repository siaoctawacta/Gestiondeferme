# AgriPilot Slice 4 — Clients, ventes, finance & rentabilité Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A sale is entered once and does the rest: a devis becomes a facture, the facture takes animals out of their lot, eggs out of the lot's production and articles out of stock, payments (comptant, partiels, à crédit) settle it, an avoir corrects it — and finance shows recettes, dépenses, marge per activity and cost and margin per lot without anyone typing a total.

**Architecture:** One deep backend module, **Documents / sales**, owns quotes, invoices, client payments and credit notes behind ONE pure action-based workflow file (`sale-workflow.ts`, the sibling of `purchase-workflow.ts`) and ONE file that talks to the ledgers (`sale-effects.ts`): it never writes a headcount, an animal status, an egg balance or a stock quantity itself — the herd ledger, the pig module, the eggs module and the stock ledger do, in the invoice's transaction. **Finance** stores nothing it can derive: recettes come from issued invoices and credit notes, dépenses from received purchases (category snapshot at reception) and lot purchase prices, the rest (salaires, énergie, transport…) from manual entries; lot costs come from `LotCostSource`s (feed and health read from the stock ledger, manual entries attributed to a lot). Screens are `ModuleConfig`s on the existing kit, extended once with a row-dependent cell.

**Tech Stack:** unchanged (NestJS 11, Prisma 7 pinned, PostgreSQL; React 19, Vite, Tailwind 4, TanStack Query).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-14-agripilot-design.md` (Documents, Herd ledger, Stock ledger, Indicators, Alerts — créance échue, Finance (owner's decision), KPI formulas — coût par animal, coût/kg, marge, coût total, Tenancy, Edit and delete, Roles — Comptable, Commercial). Reference UI: `docs/reference/screens/m_clients.png`, `m_ventes.png`, `m_finance.png`, `home.png` (Rentabilité du mois, Recettes vs dépenses), `alertes.png` (`Créance FA-0309`), `rapports.png` (Activité Avicole / Porcin / Général) and `docs/reference/screens-text.txt` (sections `m_clients`, `m_ventes`, `m_finance`, `home`, `alertes`, `rapports`). Carry-over: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-15-slice-3-carryover.md`.

## Global Constraints

- All Global Constraints of the slice 1–3 plans still apply (French UI and messages; `/api`; strict DTOs; `farmId` + indexes on farm-owned tables; `X-Farm-Id`, `all` read-only; every route exactly one of `@Public` / `@RequirePermission` / `@AnyAuthenticated`; in-transaction audit, none on a no-op; calendar dates `YYYY-MM-DD`, no future-dated operations; Decimals in DB, numbers in API; frontend queries through `useFarmQuery`, screens as `ModuleConfig`s, one form engine).
- Permission rules (in `backend/src/access/permissions.ts`), expressed only with the existing decorators: **master records** `@RequireMaster` (`update`); **daily entries** `@RequireDaily` (`create`); workflow steps `@RequirePermission(module, action)`. Module keys of this slice: `clients`, `ventes`, `finance`. Customers are master records; quotes, invoice drafts and manual finance entries are daily entries; issuing and payments need `ventes:update`; credit notes and the cancellation of an issued invoice need `ventes:validate` (grants: see Task 2 and pre-flight Q1–Q2).
- Money: `Decimal` from DTO to response; one money module `backend/src/common/money.ts` (moved from `purchases/purchase-money.ts`): a line is `round(quantity × unitPrice, 2)` (half away from zero), a document total is the **sum of rounded lines**, never `round(Σ q × p)`. Stored totals (`Purchase.amount`, `PurchaseItem.amount`, `Quote.total`, `Invoice.total`, `CreditNote.total`, line `amount`s) are written only from these functions and CHECKed in SQL (`amount = ROUND(quantity * "unitPrice", 2)` on lines). No tax, no discount: line totals and a grand total (owner's decision).
- Numbering per organisation through ONE counter table `DocumentCounter(organizationId, kind)` (renamed from `PurchaseCounter`): `DA-NNNN`, `BC-NNNN` (existing), `DV-NNNN` (devis, at creation), `FA-NNNN` (facture, **at issue** — a draft has no number, so issued numbers have no gaps), `AV-NNNN` (avoir), `RC-NNNN` (reçu, one per client payment). A counter only goes up; the counter row is locked right after the document row and before any ledger lock.
- Lock order on every sales write: the document row (`lockRow`, scoped to `ctx.farmIds`) → its status checked through `sale-workflow.ts` → the counter row (if a number is assigned) → lots (`HerdLedgerService.lockBatches`, ascending id; egg lots included) → animals (`AnimalTargetResolver.lockAnimal`, ascending id) → ONE stock-ledger call (`recordMany` / `reverseBySource`; the ledger locks last). Never two stock-ledger calls in one transaction.
- A sale line names its source and the owner decrements it: `LOT` → herd event `VENTE` (source `SALE`/invoice id); `ANIMAL` → pig exit `VENDU` (source `SALE`); `OEUFS` → the lot's egg balance (derived, owned by `eggs`); `ARTICLE` → stock `SORTIE` reason `VENTE` (source `SALE`); `SERVICE` → nothing. Ids in bodies are checked: customers and articles against the organisation, lots and animals against the active farm (400, never 404 for a body id).
- Withdrawal blocks the sale: a `LOT` or `OEUFS` line of a lot, or an `ANIMAL` line, whose `withdrawalUntil` ≥ the invoice date (inclusive, slice-3 ruling) → 400 `Le lot {code} est en délai d'attente jusqu'au {date} : vente impossible` / `L'animal {tag} est en délai d'attente jusqu'au {date} : vente impossible`. No override (pre-flight Q13).
- A validated inventory closes the past: an `ARTICLE` line dated before the latest validated count of that (farm, article) is refused by the ledger, 409 `Un inventaire validé le {date} verrouille les mouvements antérieurs de {article}` — at issue, at cancellation and on a credit-note return alike.
- Finance rules (owner's decision): a facture counts as a recette at its date, an avoir as a negative recette at its date; payments only change the créance. A received purchase counts as a dépense at its reception date, in the category snapshotted at reception; supplier payments only change the supplier balance. Salaires, énergie, eau, transport and other charges are manual entries. General charges stay under `Général` and are not spread over lots.
- **No double counting:** the farm view (recettes − dépenses, per activity, per category) never adds stock consumption (feed, medicines) — that money is already the purchase. The lot view (cost per lot) never adds purchases — it adds what the lot consumed (feed, health) at weighted-average cost, its own purchase price, and manual entries attributed to it. The two views are labelled `Marge` (farm) and `Marge du lot` (analytic); their sums are not expected to agree, and the screens say so in a hint.
- Activities (exact labels): `Avicole`, `Porcin`, `Général`. Line activity is derived: `LOT` → the lot's species, `ANIMAL` → `Porcin`, `OEUFS` → `Avicole`, `ARTICLE` / `SERVICE` → `Général`. A purchase carries its own `activity` (default `Général`).
- Statuses (exact labels and tones): invoices `Brouillon` neutral, `Émise` warning, `Partielle` warning, `Soldée` success, `Annulée` danger; quotes `Brouillon` neutral, `Envoyé` warning, `Accepté` success, `Refusé` danger, `Facturé` success, `Expiré` danger (derived: `ENVOYE` and `validUntil` < today, never stored, like `EN_RETARD`).
- New settings with defaults: `invoiceDueDays` 15 (`Échéance des factures (jours)`), `eggsPerTray` 30 (`Œufs par plateau`). Both positive integers, both snapshotted on the document (due date, `eggsPerUnit` on the line) so changing a setting never rewrites history.

## Review Focus

1. **Ledger interplay and double counting** — an invoice that sells 300 birds, 400 plateaux and 100 kg of feed writes exactly one herd event, one egg outflow and one stock exit (pieces sharing an `operationId`), all with source `SALE`/invoice id; cancelling removes exactly those; finance summary expenses do not move when the feed is consumed, lot cost does not move when the purchase is received. (Tasks 1, 3, 4, 6.)
2. **Money rounding** — per-line rounding then sum on every document; a credit note that completes a line credits exactly the line's remaining amount (2.5 × 333.33 = 833.33 credited as 416.66 + 416.67, never 416.66 twice); stored totals equal recomputed ones; payments and refunds never leave a due below 0 or above the total. (Tasks 1, 2, 5.)
3. **Cancellation and credit-note reversals** — cancelling an issued invoice restores headcount, animal status, egg balance, stock level and weighted-average cost as if it never happened, or is refused whole (closed lot, validated inventory, a later event that would go negative) with nothing written; a credit note with return re-enters goods at its own date at the sale's cost; neither can be applied twice. (Tasks 4, 5.)
4. **Permissions and tenancy** — Commercial cannot issue a credit note or read finance; Comptable cannot create quotes; Magasinier sees nothing of sales; `all` is read-only; a customer, lot, animal, article or invoice id of another organisation or farm in a body is a 400, in a path a 404; a role without `finance:read` sees no profitability on the dashboard. (Tasks 2–6.)
5. **Concurrency on stock and lots** — two invoices issued at once on a lot of 40 for 30 each, on 4 710 eggs for 100 plateaux each, on 1 000 kg for 600 kg each: exactly one 201, one 400 (`Effectif insuffisant` / `Œufs disponibles insuffisants…` / `Stock insuffisant pour …`), never a 500 or a deadlock; an issue racing a treatment on the same lot sees it or not, never half; two issues never get the same `FA` number. (Tasks 3, 4.)
